About

Client services and marketing professional in Melbourne. These are the liner notes.

For more than ten years I've worked where the client is right in front of you: leading teams, solving problems on the spot and keeping people coming back. Alongside that I've built a marketing career, with a Bachelor of Business (Marketing) and a role as Digital Marketing Coordinator. That mix is what I bring: I understand what clients need, and I know how to tell them about it.

On the marketing side I've spent more than three years on social content, SEO and paid campaigns for fintech, SaaS and fashion clients including LENSELL, Diversiview and Pruijn.

As a manager at Mile End Bagels I lead a team, plan resources against forecasts, report on performance to the business each week and build the systems that keep service consistent, like Mile End Method. Outside work I make music and build software, which is where Spagbol came from.
